THE DATA, IN PLAIN ENGLISH
Updated 12 November 2026

Original downloads are in data/raw. We keep them unchanged. Smaller tables made
for analysis are in data/processed. The website described in README.md lets
you browse charts and countries. The short report in outputs/data_audit/report.md
explains the findings and open questions.

DEMOCRACY AND ELECTIONS

V-Dem (V-Dem-CY-FullOthers-v16_csv.zip)
Democracy scores for each country and year, with uncertainty ranges. Useful
for tracking a country over time. A score is an expert estimate, not a poll.
Source: https://v-dem.net/data/the-v-dem-dataset/

Our World in Data (owid_democracy/)
Only the two Freedom House tables we use (political rights, civil liberties).
The other 268 downloaded tables were removed; index.csv still lists the full
original download with each file's URL.
Source: https://ourworldindata.org/democracy

International IDEA (idea_export_voter_turnout_database_region.xlsx)
Election turnout. Useful for seeing whether people voted. Some dates appear
to stand for a year rather than a precise day; three reported turnout rates
are above 100%. These are flagged in the working table.
Source: https://www.idea.int/data-tools/data/voter-turnout-database

PARTIES AND GOVERNMENTS

ParlGov (parlgov_2024/)
Parties, election results and cabinets. Useful for political history and
party IDs. This download does not cover the latest French governments.
Source: https://www.parlgov.org/

PPEG (ppeg_2025v1/)
More recent elections, parties and governments. Useful for the French 2024
election and 2025 government changes. Some election rows are alliances, so
their votes cannot simply be assigned to one party.
Source: https://ppeg.wzb.eu/

PopuList (The PopuList 4.0.csv)
Labels European parties as populist, far right, far left or Eurosceptic.
An unlisted party should not automatically be treated as the opposite.
Source: https://popu-list.org/

CHES (ches/)
Experts' ratings of party positions on economic, social and European issues.
Measured in survey waves, not every year. Party names and IDs need checking
before joining CHES to election votes.
Source: https://www.chesdata.eu/

Manifesto Project (manifesto_2026a/)
Measures what parties wrote in election manifestos. The newest French
manifesto in this download is from 2022. Its source terms restrict sharing
the raw data without authorization.
Source: https://manifesto-project.wzb.eu/

PUBLIC OPINION

EVS/WVS joint survey (evs_wvs_joint_2017_2022_v5/)
Answers from individual people, including 1,870 French interviews in 2018.
Useful for confidence in parliament, government and parties. Missing answers
and survey weights need care. The raw data have sharing restrictions.
Source: https://www.worldvaluessurvey.org/WVSEVSjoint2017.jsp

Eurobarometer (eurobarometer_std105_2026/)
Published percentages from European opinion surveys. This download includes
a longer trust trend and a recent France comparison. It is not a file of
individual responses; question wording matters when comparing surveys.
Source: https://europa.eu/eurobarometer/surveys/detail/3613

CEVIPOF (cevipof_bcp_2026/)
A 2026 report on political trust. We copied a few published figures and kept
their question and page numbers. We do not have its respondent data.
Source: https://www.sciencespo.fr/cevipof/

ESS (ess/)
Only a questionnaire and weighting guide are in this folder. There is no
respondent file yet, so ESS is not part of the current analysis.
Source: https://ess.sikt.no/en/data-builder/

ECONOMIC CONTEXT

Eurostat (eurostat_macro_annual/)
Annual growth, unemployment, inflation and public-debt figures. Useful for
describing the 2008 financial crisis and other background conditions. These
country figures alone do not show what caused a change in trust or voting.
Source: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/

UNUSED DOWNLOADS (shocks/)

World Bank GDP growth, EM-DAT disaster deaths and UCDP organized violence were
downloaded for a shock timeline that is not part of the current website or
pipeline (src/build_shocks.py is not run). They are kept for later work.
Sources: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG
https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/natural-disasters-deaths
https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/
