E energy-price shock. Rule: year-on-year rate of change of the HICP energy component (Eurostat prc_hicp_manr, NRG) above 15.60%, the 90th percentile of EU-member country-months from Jan 2001, France excluded (specification: top 10%). A spell is a run of months above it, gaps of up to 2 months allowed. Sliders: energy rise 15.6% to 60% year on year (mean over the spell), duration 1 to 29 months. Reading = energy_shock + energy_yoy * (yoy - 15.60) + duration_months * (months - 1) + gas_share * (14.8 - 20.21) (France's centred gas share: -5.37). Ranges: wild cluster bootstrap; add the half-width in e_conformal.csv to speak about one future episode. Threshold fixed on the months to Dec 2025, before the 2026 surge; data run to 2026-10. Spells still running at the end of the data are not used for estimation. Estimated without France; waves from Mar 2001; baseline = mean of the two waves before the spell, after wave starts at least 30 days after it.
