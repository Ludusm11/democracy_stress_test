    How to read: trust is the Eurobarometer share who “tend to trust”: the average of the two waves before the episode → the lowest wave during it (or just after it). “EU” is how the EU average moved between the same waves. Turnout and parties compare the last election before the episode with the last one during it (or up to a year after). “Parties” is the effective number of parties: higher means votes are spread more thinly.
