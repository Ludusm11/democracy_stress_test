# Standard Eurobarometer 105, Spring 2026

Source survey: https://europa.eu/eurobarometer/surveys/detail/3613  
Data portal: https://data.europa.eu/data/datasets/s3613_105_2_std105_eng?locale=en  
GESIS study: https://www.gesis.org/en/eurobarometer-data-service/data-and-documentation/standard-special-eb/study-overview/eurobarometer-1052-za9144-march-april-2026

Fieldwork: 12 March to 5 April 2026. France is included. The files downloaded here are **weighted aggregate tables**, not respondent-level microdata:

- `STD105_volume_A.xlsx`: country and EU results by question.
- `STD105_volume_AP.xlsx`: the same country results compared with Standard Eurobarometer 104.1 (Autumn 2025).
- `STD105_volume_D.zip`: longer trend tables; includes separate workbooks for `QA6_2`, `QA6_10`, and `QA6_11`.
- `EB105_2_basic_questionnaire_EN_FR.pdf`: English and French question text from GESIS.

## France indicators for the first prototype

The percentages below are the published weighted shares of all respondents, including those answering "Don't know" in the base. Read the percentages from the English-labelled rows in `STD105_volume_AP.xlsx`. The France pair is in columns AA (EB 104.1) and AB (EB 105.2).

| Indicator | Sheet / row | Autumn 2025 | Spring 2026 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Trust in political parties | `QA6_2!AA13:AB13` | 7% | 12% |
| Trust in national government | `QA6_10!AA13:AB13` | 9% | 21% |
| Trust in national parliament | `QA6_11!AA13:AB13` | 12% | 23% |
| Satisfied with democracy in France | `SD18a!AA23:AB23` | 37% | 56% |

`STD105_volume_A.xlsx` gives the Spring 2026 results alone. Its France column is O for these sheets. The France total in these tables is 1,024 weighted responses. The `SD18a` result combines "very satisfied" and "fairly satisfied" as provided by the source.

These are measures of public opinion at the time of each survey. Do not present the difference between two waves as the effect of a particular government event or a simulated stress scenario without a separate research design. For respondent-level analysis, GESIS lists Eurobarometer 105.2 (ZA9144) as an archive pre-release; the Commission portal files here contain aggregates.
